Channel Publisher

A private publishing tool for one creator's own YouTube channels.

Channel Publisher is a personal automation tool. Its owner uses it to publish their own videos to their own YouTube channels, and to read those channels' analytics so future videos can be improved. It is not a public service: no one else can sign up for it or use it, and it never acts on anyone else's channel.

What it does

  • Uploads the owner's finished videos and Shorts, and schedules when they go public.
  • Sets each video's title, description, chapters, captions, thumbnail and playlists.
  • Reads the owner's channel analytics (views, watch time, audience retention, impressions and click-through rate) to produce a private weekly report.
  • Reads comments on the owner's videos so replies can be drafted for the owner to approve.

What it does not do

  • It does not access any channel or account that the owner does not own.
  • It does not sell, share or advertise with any data.
  • It does not collect data from viewers or other users.
